Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · Website privacy notice

What It Costs ("we," "us," "our") operates this informational website. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the site or contact us. This policy describes the current static website only—not a future app, analytics dashboard, or YouTube API integration.

1. Information collected by this website

We do not operate sign-up forms, user accounts, advertising pixels, behavioral analytics, or marketing trackers on this website. We do not intentionally set first-party cookies or use local storage in the website code.

However, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and network intermediaries may process technical information required to deliver the site, such as IP addresses, requests, browser details, and security-related logs, according to their own policies. We do not claim to control those systems or their retention periods.

2. Information you choose to send

If you email us, we may receive your email address, name, message, and any information you include. We use this information solely to respond, evaluate collaboration inquiries, manage correspondence, or address legitimate rights and safety requests. Do not email passwords, authentication tokens, financial information, or other sensitive information.

3. How we use and retain information

We do not sell contact information. Emails are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, recordkeeping, or applicable legal obligations, then deleted or archived in accordance with the email provider's capabilities. You may request that we delete your correspondence, subject to applicable exceptions.

4. Third-party sites

Links to YouTube or other services lead to independent sites with their own privacy practices. This website does not embed the YouTube player and does not connect to your Google or YouTube account. The hosting infrastructure is provided by GitHub. Review GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy for their respective handling of data.

5. Children

This website provides general-audience information and is not designed to collect information from children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13 through this website. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent personal data may contact us to request review or removal.

6. Your choices

You may browse this website without creating an account. You may request access, correction, or deletion of information that you voluntarily emailed us, where applicable. We cannot directly edit hosting-provider technical logs or third-party platform data.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date above. In particular, we must revise this policy before adding analytics, forms, advertising tools, account sign-in, Google OAuth, YouTube API features, or AI-agent integrations that handle personal or account information.

8. Contact

For privacy requests, please use the public business contact on our Contact page.
